## C1 — Action-Fiber Set Diffusion

- `dedup_key`: `geometry_action_fiber_set_diffusion`
- Problem: a single demonstration is one member of a geometry-dependent set of valid chunks, so behavior cloning collapses collision-free approach/contact modes.
- Mechanism: (1) build unordered target sets of rollback-verified viable chunks from demonstrations, perturbations, and short-horizon planning; (2) train a geometry-conditioned diffusion policy to emit K chunks jointly with permutation-invariant unbalanced optimal transport that penalizes missed viable modes and infeasible mass; (3) sample one mode and regenerate after re-observation, without a classifier or WAM ranker.
- Collision note: RPDiff models multimodal terminal relational poses; AML 2605.11832 calls clean-action prediction an action manifold; neither supervises set-valued temporally extended feasible support.
- Kill: no viable-mode recall and success gain over matched-sample diffusion, or gain disappears after matching sample count.
- Feasibility: simulator collision queries and cached planner rollouts; frozen VLA plus diffusion head/LoRA on 4×A100.

## C2 — Bifurcation-Regularized Action Diffusion

- `dedup_key`: `geometry_bifurcation_regularized_action_diffusion`
- Problem: clearance/articulation changes can create or destroy action modes, but ordinary conditioning has no signal about support bifurcations.
- Mechanism: (1) sweep geometry across feasibility thresholds and label path signature/contact schedule; (2) couple noise across adjacent geometries and use unbalanced transport to move surviving modes while allowing mass to vanish only at signed-clearance crossings; (3) regularize reverse diffusion so mode birth/death is local to the bottleneck.
- Collision note: RPDiff captures multimodal placement but not birth/death of full action modes along controlled geometry sweeps; GEAR-VLA changes representation, not action-support topology.
- Kill: no improvement in mode count/mass/success near held-out thresholds over independently conditioned diffusion, or degradation away from thresholds.
- Feasibility: procedural sweeps, several hundred thousand short chunks, moderate action diffusion adapter on 4×A100.

## C3 — Counterfactual Feasibility Repair Flow

- `dedup_key`: `counterfactual_geometry_action_repair_flow`
- Problem: a frozen VLA proposal may be nearly correct but geometry-incompatible; resampling/ranking wastes its useful structure.
- Mechanism: (1) transfer successful chunks into minimally edited geometries and optimize minimal feasible corrections with collision gradients; (2) train a conditional residual flow from geometry and proposed chunk to correction distributions, with identity and edit-cycle losses; (3) transport the frozen VLA's proposal into viable support in one pass.
- Collision note: MT/CASC only diagnose/audit pairs; DreamSteer ranks complete proposals; constrained diffusion and trajectory repair are close mechanisms.
- Kill: no advantage over matched-latency resampling, analytic signed-distance projection, and residual BC, or corruption of already viable chunks.
- Feasibility: cache simulator optimization targets; compact short-chunk flow on 4×A100.

## C4 — Counterfactual Boundary Score Matching

- `dedup_key`: `counterfactual_geometry_boundary_score_matching`
- Problem: positive demonstrations do not identify which visible geometry makes an action valid, while naive negatives suppress legitimate alternative modes.
- Mechanism: (1) create minimal geometry edits that flip feasibility of the same chunk and retain alternative positives for both variants; (2) attract the chunk under its valid geometry and repel it under the counterfactual only at collision-localized timesteps; (3) use an alternative-positive envelope so repulsion preserves other viable modes.
- Collision note: CASC uses nuisance-invariant pairs for offline drift; MT is evaluation; RoCoDA edits task-irrelevant state and SE(3)-transforms positives; CofactVLA uses a language-masked counterfactual branch. None uses minimally feasibility-flipping geometry as localized score supervision.
- Kill: no collision reduction on unseen minimal edits while retaining alternative-mode entropy and nominal success versus positive-only and random-negative training.
- Feasibility: simulator edits and collision timestamps; LoRA/action-head tuning on 4×A100.

## C5 — Barrier-Distilled Counterfactual WAM

- `dedup_key`: `counterfactual_wam_barrier_distillation`
- Problem: test-time WAM reranking is slow and does not teach the base generator functional geometry.
- Mechanism: (1) train a compact WAM on native and cross-geometry rollouts to predict signed distance, penetration, and contact impulse; (2) form a differentiable horizon-wide chance barrier and backpropagate it through action chunks; (3) distill corrected actions into the VLA and discard the WAM at deployment.
- Collision note: DreamSteer ranks at deployment; World Pilot steers with WAM priors; PhysReflect checks feasibility online; this proposal distills continuous counterfactual barrier fields into a single-pass actor.
- Kill: predicted fields fail to order risk or distillation gives no matched-latency collision/success gain.
- Feasibility: short-horizon latent WAM, cached simulator fields, task-wise adapter training on 4×A100.

## C6 — Geometry-Conditioned Backward Viability Tubes

- `dedup_key`: `geometry_backward_viability_tube_policy`
- Problem: a locally collision-free action can enter a geometry-specific dead end from which the goal is unreachable.
- Mechanism: (1) train reverse-dynamics diffusion from successful goal-contact states backward through collision-free transitions, conditioned on visible geometry; (2) compress reverse samples into a geometry-conditioned viability tube and condition the forward decoder on its current intersection; (3) if outside, generate recovery toward the nearest reachable tube segment.
- Collision note: BaRC/reverse curricula expand training initial states; RPDiff emits terminal poses; reachability-weighted offline RL scores reachability. This proposal uses a multimodal backward tube as an online action representation for vision-language manipulation.
- Kill: no reduction in entry to locally safe but goal-dead branches relative to forward diffusion and collision MPC, or oracle tubes do not help.
- Feasibility: simulator rollback and successful trajectories; low-dimensional reverse model plus VLA adapter on 4×A100.

## C7 — Recovery-Support Diffusion for Physical Refusal

- `dedup_key`: `physical_infeasibility_recovery_support_diffusion`
- Problem: impossible visible geometry has no generative support in standard VLA training, so the policy continues forceful task motion.
- Mechanism: (1) create paired feasible/impossible instances and collect sets of safe hold/retract/reobserve/regrasp trajectories for impossible cases rather than refusal labels; (2) train one geometry-conditioned trajectory diffusion whose support moves from task-progress to recovery across feasibility sweeps; (3) add absorbing safe hold so replanning cannot resume forceful motion until vision again supports reachability.
- Collision note: Act/Think/Abstain is a scene-level complexity classifier; PhysReflect-VLA checks candidate feasibility and reflects; DreamSteer chooses among offered proposals. This candidate learns recovery/refusal as trajectory support and can resume when geometry becomes feasible.
- Kill: cannot suppress damaging task motion on impossible cases while retaining near-boundary feasible success, or cannot resume after feasibility is restored.
- Feasibility: procedural impossible cases and scripted/MPC recovery; only action head/adapter training on 4×A100.

## C8 — Executable Relation-Edit Action Codec

- `dedup_key`: `executable_relation_edit_action_codec`
- Problem: low-level tokens bind behavior to training geometry, while semantic skills omit interacting surface pairs and continuous contact motion.
- Mechanism: (1) convert demonstrations into typed relation edits and surface anchors; (2) emit a relation-edit token, anchor identities, and event-normalized local twist spline, decoded from live geometry; (3) enforce a graph-transition/action cycle so reconstructed actions realize the edit.
- Collision note: WALL-WM uses semantic events as training units; GSR predicts scene-graph transitions; GEAR-VLA learns geometry-aware actions; contact-graph planning searches edits. The proposed codec makes typed relation edit plus surface-anchored spline the executable action representation.
- Kill: token accuracy improves without success, or removing graph-action cycle changes nothing across held-out insertion/hanging/articulation.
- Feasibility: simulator relation graphs, frozen VLM, tokenizer/graph decoder/DiT adapter on 4×A100.

## C9 — Event-Normalized Geometry Spline Policy

- `dedup_key`: `event_normalized_geometry_spline`
- Problem: fixed-duration chunks conflate geometric path and speed and often cut through contact transitions.
- Mechanism: (1) segment at persistent visible relation changes and normalize each event to phase [0,1]; (2) predict the next relation edit and surface-frame B-spline plus a separate monotone time warp; (3) execute with phase feedback to the represented relation boundary.
- Collision note: WALL-WM already has event-grounded pretraining and variable-length event execution. Delta is explicit factorization of relation-bounded geometric path from physical time in local surface coordinates.
- Kill: no sample-efficiency or held-out geometry gain under speed-randomized demos, or time-warp head removable without effect.
- Feasibility: offline simulator relation boundaries; lightweight spline/timing heads on frozen VLA, 4×A100.

## C10 — Contact-Topology Path Diffusion

- `dedup_key`: `contact_topology_path_diffusion`
- Problem: terminal pose/affordance does not specify ordered contact modes for insertion, sliding, hanging, and articulation.
- Mechanism: (1) collapse trajectories into persistent contact-topology graph-edit paths; (2) generate multimodal paths with conditional discrete diffusion; (3) decode each transition with a shared surface-coordinate inverse-dynamics model and compose dense execution.
- Collision note: RPDiff gives terminal relational poses; graph planners search edits; GSR predicts scene transitions; CoToGrasp conditions grasp synthesis on contact topology. This is a learned multimodal manipulation contact-mode sequence used as an action plan.
- Kill: no transfer benefit on held-out geometries requiring different intermediate contact modes, or oracle topology paths do not improve the dense controller.
- Feasibility: simulator collision labels; compact graph diffusion and per-transition decoder on 4×A100.

## C11 — Single-Chunk Geometry Repair Field

- `dedup_key`: `single_chunk_geometry_repair_field`
- Problem: the coarse skill is correct but approach, clearance, or contact point is incompatible with current geometry.
- Mechanism: (1) perturb expert chunks/geometry near contact and retain intended relation edit plus constraint violations; (2) learn an SE(3)-equivariant residual field that minimally deforms one trajectory while satisfying collision, reachability, and smoothness; (3) attach to frozen VLA with identity regularization.
- Collision note: DreamSteer reranks, RPDiff emits placements, GEAR-VLA learns geometry-aware representations, and surface-constrained policies project intentions. Delta is minimal relation-edit-conditioned deformation of a single VLA chunk.
- Kill: no advantage over untouched VLA and generic residual policy on calibrated errors, or only smoothing without success recovery.
- Feasibility: synthetic supervision and point-cloud residual network on 4×A100.

## C12 — Counterfactual Topology-Equivariant Policy Training

- `dedup_key`: `counterfactual_topology_equivariant_training`
- Problem: a policy should be invariant/equivariant when geometry preserves the required relation path, but change action when geometry changes that path.
- Mechanism: (1) generate matched geometry interventions with expert relation paths/actions; (2) use transported paired cross-reconstruction when the path is preserved, and condition on edited relation-token sequences when it changes; (3) fine-tune a VLA adapter with paired reconstruction and topology consistency for direct action decoding.
- Collision note: MT/CASC construct controlled pairs to test/audit; RoCoDA augments irrelevant subsets and SE(3)-equivariant positives; this uses topology-preserving and topology-changing functional interventions as supervised equivariance constraints on executable action representations.
- Kill: cannot jointly reduce inappropriate drift on preserving edits and improve necessary adaptation on topology-changing edits versus unpaired domain randomization.
- Feasibility: procedural pairs, expert actions, LoRA plus action-transport module on 4×A100.

## C13 — Intended–Realized Relation Reconciliation

- `dedup_key`: `intended_realized_relation_reconciliation`
- Problem: after imperfect contact, a policy repeats the nominal skill although the intended relation edit was missed or a spurious contact appeared.
- Mechanism: (1) train a joint WAM to emit intended next relation edit and predicted realized relation graph; (2) encode missing/premature/spurious edit residual and train a short generative reconciliation action on perturbed demos; (3) inject reconciliation latent into continuous decoder each control update.
- Collision note: EA-WM decodes event states and scores candidate futures; DreamSteer/DreamTrajectory select candidates/alignment. Delta is a structured intended-versus-realized relation residual that conditions a generative correction policy.
- Kill: structured residual does not beat raw WAM-latent conditioning or fails on missed grasp, false support, shallow insertion, premature release.
- Feasibility: small relation decoder and correction DiT on frozen visual/WAM features, parallel simulator perturbations, 4×A100.

## C14 — Surface-Pair Gauge Action Representation

- `dedup_key`: `surface_pair_gauge_action_representation`
- Problem: base/end-effector-frame actions remain tied to object scale/layout because they do not represent motion relative to both interacting surfaces.
- Mechanism: (1) identify an ordered functional surface pair and construct a local gauge from centroids, normals, tangents, separation, and curvature; (2) encode an event trajectory as gauge-covariant twist-spline coefficients plus gripper events and train the VLA to predict patch pair/coefficients; (3) instantiate motion analytically on unseen geometry with a small dynamics residual.
- Collision note: GEAR-VLA canonicalizes actions to end-effector and injects 3D features; AffordanceVLA predicts where/how; RPDiff uses local geometry for terminal poses. This represents a complete event trajectory in a dynamic two-surface relational frame.
- Kill: no extrapolative geometry transfer over base-frame/end-effector-relative representations, or either surface can be removed without degradation.
- Feasibility: surface frames from simulator meshes/RGB-D; patch selector, coefficient decoder, residual controller on 4×A100.
