# Refinement Report — WitnessWAM

The initial passive-witness concept was narrowed from an ambiguous twin/competing-risk design into one implementable claim: schedule updates to an external progress-memory relation using a single action-conditioned opportunity hazard during an immutable five-action continuation.

Five review rounds removed causal and evaluation shortcuts: physical common-parent aliases replace ideal twins; truth-changing continuations stay in the failure denominator; the continuation envelope is checkable from robot proprioception and queued physical commands; four paired π0.5 decode keys are frozen without replacement; all physical actions and prompts are locked to the chunk boundary; and parent lineages are the statistical unit.

Most importantly, the final design can be killed by two smaller explanations. A matched action-conditioned visibility hazard tests whether the method only forecasts hand withdrawal/visibility. A matched full-history boundary verifier tests whether buffering the five observations is sufficient without explicit witness timing. The proposed mechanism survives only if it exceeds both by at least 10% per retained family with lineage-bootstrap confidence.

The method is READY for final novelty re-audit, not yet authorized for experiment launch. Novelty must remain strictly above 7.0 after considering the refined exact mechanism.
