# Experiment Plan

**Problem:** Does a valid predictive future-latent gradient shape a frozen VLA action-interface representation that closed-loop control locally uses?  
**Method thesis:** A valid-target gradient must pass a predictive gate, beat stop-gradient and one fixed matched deranged-target algorithm across training seeds, and only then pass a placebo-relative predictive-projector intervention.  
**Date:** 2026-08-07  
**Method status:** READY 9.0/10, same-family provisional  
**Paper writing:** out of scope

## Claim Map

| Claim | Why it matters | Minimum convincing evidence | Blocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| C0 Predictive validity | prevents current-state/action/task shortcuts | full frozen decoder beats strongest controls and both shuffle gates on untouched episodes | B1 |
| C1 Controlled algorithmic effect | isolates valid correspondence from no auxiliary update and one matched wrong correspondence | Stage-1 A−B and A−C seed gates, ≥10pp, no task reversal, valid C | B2, B3 |
| C2 Placebo-relative reliance | tests whether a predeclared treatment-associated predictive component carries the gain | locked P/Q eligibility plus Stage-2 contrast, ≥10pp and ≥50% gap attenuation | B4 |
| Anti-claim | rules out seed/output collapse and harmful C | per-seed output/gradient diagnostics; C≥B−5pp; no outcome-adaptive selection | all |

## Paper Storyline

- **Must prove:** C0 before policy training; C1 before any mechanism rollout; C2 only conditionally.
- **Supporting diagnostics:** action L1/diversity/effective rank, gradient cosine/norm, per-task failures, timing.
- **Intentionally cut:** new architecture, backbone LoRA, action-head tuning, pixel/video generation, planner/RL, more horizons/teachers/tasks, real robot, SOTA comparison.

## Experiment Blocks

### B0 — Interface, isolation and parity

- **Claim tested:** implementation preserves the frozen policy and data semantics.
- **Data:** fixed tasks 3/6; personal LIBERO; no shared path.
- **Checks:** clean commit/checkpoint hashes; t/t+8 validity; episode manifests; DINO strict local load; identity/direct-cache/hot-switch action error <1e-6; task/init-state/evaluator semantics.
- **Success:** every deterministic gate passes.
- **Failure:** repair implementation; no model or policy result may be opened.
- **Priority:** MUST-RUN.

### B1 — Held-out future-latent predictive validity

- **Claim tested:** the frozen q(m,a) carries future association beyond current-state/task/action shortcuts.
- **Systems:** full; state-only; action-only; current-DINO+action; additive; nuisance/task-progress/zero; action/representation derangements.
- **Split:** D_head train/val/strict-test only.
- **Metrics:** joint64/main32 nMSE, relative gain, episode-bootstrap lower bound, derangement sensitivity.
- **Success:** exact ≥5%/LCB gates in FINAL_PROPOSAL.
- **Failure:** H1 NO-RUN before adapter policy experiment.
- **Cost:** pilot; charged to 2 GPUh pilot cap.
- **Priority:** MUST-RUN.

### B2 — Matched A/B/C training and untouched offline eligibility

- **Claim tested:** correct future gradient changes the interface predictively under matched data/steps/dose.
- **Systems:** A valid; B stop-grad/action-only adapter; C fixed stratified matched derangement.
- **Runs:** seed0 complete triplet first; remaining 4 or 9 only after budget choice.
- **Metrics:** action/future loss; dose error; nonfinite/cap fallback; gradient cosine/norm; clip/update; adapter residual; D_post A vs identity/B/C nMSE.
- **Success:** C matching/training gates; every selected seed Stage-1 eligibility.
- **Failure:** no seed replacement; any Stage-1 eligibility failure forbids Stage 1.
- **Priority:** MUST-RUN.

### B3 — Stage-1 paired closed-loop effect

- **Claim tested:** valid correspondence gradient algorithm has a large, seed-consistent local effect.
- **Checkpoint:** treatment-blind-selected from exact 1k/5k/10k set.
- **Tasks:** fixed LIBERO-10 IDs 3 and 6.
- **Conditions:** A/B/C intact.
- **Runs:** 300 rollouts, common initial states.
- **Primary metrics:** seed-level A−B and A−C success differences.
- **Success:** 9/10 or 5/5 positive; pooled ≥10pp; each task ≥0; C≥B−5pp; all audits valid.
- **Failure:** report negative/underpowered local result; forbid B4 environment rollouts.
- **Priority:** MUST-RUN after gates.

### B4 — Conditional Stage-2 predictive reliance

- **Claim tested:** the treatment-associated predictive projector is more behaviorally load-bearing than one empirical/action-output-matched Q.
- **Offline prereq:** every selected seed passes locked fold-4 P/Q equivalence, support, parity and eligibility.
- **Conditions:** A_pred removal vs A_Q removal.
- **Runs:** 200 conditional rollouts, same initial states.
- **Primary metric:** seed-level SR(A_Q)−SR(A_pred).
- **Success:** sign/10pp/no-task-reversal; A_Q within5pp intact A; A_pred erases≥50% A−B without undershooting B by >10pp.
- **Failure:** C1 may remain; no representation reliance claim.
- **Priority:** CONDITIONAL MUST-RUN.

### B5 — Integrity and claim audit

- **Claim tested:** only supported claims survive.
- **Checks:** manifests/hashes, all failed/missing runs, GPUh, seed exclusions, outcome order, leakage, matched budgets, task-level results, no test tuning.
- **Tools:** experiment-audit then result-to-claim.
- **Success:** integrity pass and claim mapped to highest passed stage.
- **Priority:** MUST-RUN.

## Statistical Protocol

- Training seed is the primary unit.
- 10-seed preferred: at least 9 positive among all 10; ties count non-positive; exact one-sided p=.0107.
- 5-seed fallback: 5/5 positive; p=.03125; label exploratory/provisional.
- A−B and A−C form an intersection-union gate.
- Common tasks and initial states are within-seed paired repeats, not additional training replicates.
- Report pooled and per-task point estimates, seed table, descriptive seed-cluster bootstrap intervals.
- No outcome-driven changes to checkpoint, task, states, seeds, rank, Q, thresholds or rollout count.
- Missing/infrastructure policy follows the frozen evaluator rule; no silent exclusions.
