## Three-level verdict

### 1. Technical executability: GO

- The environment runs and restores deterministically.
- RGB-D, instance masks and robot-only kinematics are available.
- The P1 collector and geometry implementation exist locally and remotely.
- The proposed first model is below 100k parameters; compute is not a bottleneck.

Remaining technical uncertainty is whether the scripted approach actually creates enough contact-mediated preserved/invalidated outcomes. P1 is designed to test exactly this.

### 2. Scientific identifiability: GO, conditional on P1 gates

The comparison is identifiable only if:

- the same command families contain both preserved and invalidated outcomes;
- command-only balanced accuracy is at most `0.60`;
- trace shuffling is within matched strata;
- labels, simulator states and post-action observations remain outside transition inputs;
- robot kinematics is not numerically redundant with commands.

If these fail after reasonable pre-state-margin and push-geometry repair, the trace-conditioned claim is not established and the line should stop.

### 3. Positive/publishable result: UNKNOWN, not guaranteed

No current result shows that realized kinematics improves over observation-only PointMap or command-only input. E0 only establishes representation headroom. A defensible paper requires E1 to beat the strongest no-trace/command baseline and later improve typed rollback or final task success. The architecture alone is not sufficiently novel.
