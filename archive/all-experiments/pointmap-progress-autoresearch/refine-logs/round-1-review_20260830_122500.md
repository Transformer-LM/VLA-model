## Senior Method Review: GeoRetract

The proposal is honest and well scoped for a falsification pilot, but it is not yet a coherent top-venue method claim. The dominant problem is causal identifiability: the current controlled interventions can establish the value of typed historical rollback and PointMap geometry, but cannot establish that an executed-action-conditioned transition is necessary.

### Critical blockers

1. “Executed action” is undefined. The method must distinguish commanded VLA action, simulator-applied action, realized robot trajectory/gripper/contact/proprioception, and resulting environment transition. If only the command exists, call it command-conditioned.
2. A fully hidden exogenous invalidation with no visible, proprioceptive, contact, or event evidence is unidentifiable. The correct output is unknown/re-observe, not confident retraction.
3. The temporal interface blends an action-conditioned transition prior with a discriminative before/after verifier. These must be separated.
4. Teleports, drops, removals, and relation swaps support rollback utility and PointMap correctness, not the action-conditioned claim. Action shuffle cannot repair this mismatch.
5. Novelty remains mechanism-light and outcome-contingent: a learned transition head can still look like POT-VLA plus CheckVLA plus a ledger.
6. `preserved/invalidated` are ontic states, while `unobservable` is epistemic and `wrong-relation` is an address error. They should not be one softmax.
7. Restrict the first study to containment/support (`inside/on`), not `grasp/open`.
8. Separate verifier correctness from recovery-policy competence in closed loop.

### Minimal revision requested

Maintain a scalar relation-validity belief plus separate visibility/reliability. Predict an action-conditioned transition prior from a frozen PointMap state and a realized trace, then fuse it with a shared POT-style observation likelihood through a fixed or calibrated log-odds update. Train only the transition head. Use paired simulator physics rollouts, real VLA-generated or replayed chunks, command-only versus realized-trace conditioning, held-out actions/tasks, action shuffle, and identifiability controls. Arbitrary external disturbances remain negative controls.

### Drift warning

Do not turn the study into a generic PointMap memory, active-subtask verifier, generic failure detector, recovery-policy contribution, full visual WAM, or perturbation benchmark unrelated to the conditioned action.
