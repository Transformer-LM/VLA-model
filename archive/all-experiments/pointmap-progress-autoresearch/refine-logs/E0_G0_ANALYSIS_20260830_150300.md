## What was repaired

The learned Oracle and PointMap heads now use fixed-scale task-invariant motion, relative-motion, visibility and missingness features. Absolute positions, quaternions, raw pixel counts and family-fitted z-normalization were removed. Every learned run stores complete validation probabilities, threshold candidates, the selected threshold, test probabilities and training history. Aggregation restores the original float32 prediction dtype and independently replays thresholds and metrics.

## Claim ceiling and unresolved risks

The strongest provisional statement is: on 360 controlled samples spanning five LIBERO task definitions, with simulator instance masks and metric depth, a fixed-size object-relative PointMap head preserves substantially more cross-family relation-event signal than the matched full-frame Depth and RGB heads while using a much smaller verifier input tensor.

This result does **not** establish:

- learned object discovery, addressing or tracking;
- robustness without simulator masks or with estimated real-world PointMaps;
- action-conditioned counterfactual rollout;
- value from realized kinematic traces;
- WAM necessity over a direct non-dynamics scorer;
- π0.5 improvement, runtime correction, recovery or closed-loop success;
- real-robot performance.

The high Depth/RGB test FRR despite validation-FRR selection also shows severe held-family calibration shift. E1 must keep risk-coverage and per-family calibration visible rather than relying only on aggregate F1.
