## Key findings

1. **Observation:** the registered G0 gate fails four of five conditions; only the 10x input-size condition passes.  
   **Interpretation:** current E0 provides no PointMap representation-headroom evidence.  
   **Implication:** E1/B2 cannot start under the preregistered plan.  
   **Next step:** repair the Oracle and rerun the complete matched grid under a new shared provenance hash.

2. **Observation:** the learned Oracle performs worse than RGB and Depth despite the raw simulator state being almost perfectly separable. The independent audit found a task-invariant rule with 359/360 correct and macro-F1 0.99655.  
   **Interpretation:** the failed Oracle is a feature/training-generalization failure, not a lack of simulator information or a metric error. Raw absolute pose, quaternion and visibility-count features create severe held-family normalization shift.  
   **Implication:** the current G0 cannot distinguish representation failure from a broken upper-bound learner.  
   **Next step:** replace the Oracle with task-invariant relative features plus a deterministic held-family sanity probe.

3. **Observation:** PointMap is much smaller and its head is faster, but its learned classifier is less accurate and less calibrated.  
   **Interpretation:** compression alone does not preserve task-relevant decision information under the current learner and held-family split.  
   **Implication:** “PointMap replaces Depth” is unsupported; PointMap remains an enabling representation, not the scientific contribution.  
   **Next step:** separately test a direct invariant relation probe and a learned invariant probe before deciding whether to retain PointMap in the main method.

4. **Observation:** fold-level uncertainty is large for every learned visual representation; PointMap macro-F1 95% CI is 0.2197 and test FRR reaches 0.43 on average.  
   **Interpretation:** family transfer dominates seed noise, and a single aggregate mean hides strong task dependence.  
   **Implication:** future experiments must retain held-family reporting and cannot claim robust cross-task generalization from this pilot.

5. **Observation:** 12 invalidated events have no post target visibility, while unobservable events also hide the target.  
   **Interpretation:** two-frame visual geometry alone can be observationally ambiguous for “relation invalidated but target hidden” versus “relation preserved but target hidden.”  
   **Implication:** this is the concrete setting in which realized action/kinematic trace could add information, but E0 does not test that claim.  
   **Next step:** only after repaired G0, build matched no-trace, commanded-action, realized-trace and shuffled-trace conditions on these ambiguity pairs.

## Gate conclusion

Scientific verdict: FAIL.  
Integrity verdict: WARN, with no fabrication or phantom-result concern.  
Allowed claim: the controlled E0/G0 diagnostic completed and failed; no representation-headroom conclusion is supported.
