# Research Proposal: Trace-Conditioned Historical Relation Filter

**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Scope:** historical non-active `inside/on` relations  
**Contribution:** attributable transition-prior versus observation-evidence study; not a generally new WAM architecture

## Problem

A long-horizon VLA may complete and commit an object relation such as `inside(cup, drawer)`, move to a later subtask, and then invalidate that old relation through a later action. RGB progress memory can retain stale completion. A current 3D predicate can correct visible errors, but becomes indecisive when the post-action object geometry is partly missing or aliased.

The claim is deliberately narrow: on a frozen post-action observation-aliasing population, does the robot's realized kinematic execution trace add conditional value to an otherwise identical object-relative PointMap belief filter?

Fully hidden exogenous changes with no visual, proprioceptive, contact, or event evidence are unidentifiable. The correct behavior is re-observation, not confident retraction.

## Belief filter

For a committed relation \(r_{ij}\), maintain

\[
q_t=P(z_t^{ij}=valid\mid h_t).
\]

A small transition head predicts the pre-observation prior

\[
q^-_{t+H}=T_\theta(q_t,\phi_t^{ij},\tau_{t:t+H},r_{ij}).
\]

The frozen post-action geometry model supplies a visibility-conditioned log-likelihood ratio

\[
\Lambda_r(o,v)=\log\frac{p(o\mid z=valid,v,r)}{p(o\mid z=invalid,v,r)},
\]

and the posterior is

\[
\operatorname{logit}q_{t+H}=\operatorname{logit}q^-_{t+H}+\Lambda_r(o_{t+H},v_{t+H}).
\]

Validation-frozen thresholds map the posterior to typed retract, re-observe, or continue. Retraction affects only the invalidated historical relation; it does not globally reset task memory.

## Information boundary

All transition traces end at the final applied control before the post-action PointMap is observed:

- \(\tau^{cmd}\): commanded 7D controls;
- \(\tau^{kin}\), the claim-bearing trace: applied controls plus robot-only end-effector, gripper, and joint proprioception;
- \(\tau^{fb}\), appendix-only: \(\tau^{kin}\) plus contact/force/event feedback, named execution-feedback-conditioned.

Forbidden transition inputs are post-action RGB/depth/PointMaps, simulator object state or velocity, relation/success labels, future-derived identity, and feedback outside \(\tau^{fb}\). A contact-only improvement cannot support the kinematic-transition claim.

## Deterministic 54D pre-action PointMap summary

PointMaps use robot-base-aligned world axes and are translated by the target robust centroid. Points are reduced deterministically: 5 mm voxelization, lexicographic voxel order, the point nearest each voxel center, then evenly spaced fixed indices if more than 128 points remain.

Each object contributes 20 values:

1. present mask and clipped visible-count fraction (2);
2. robust centroid in the target-centered base frame (3);
3. coordinate q10/q50/q90 (9);
4. descending covariance eigenvalues (3);
5. q90-q10 extents (3).

The target/reference blocks contribute 40 values. The pair block contributes 14: signed and absolute centroid displacement (6), minimum cross-cloud distance (1), three robust-box overlap ratios (3), inside margin plus validity mask (2), and support margin plus validity mask (2).

If the target is empty, its block is zero; the reference retains only present/count and its other 18 values are zero; the full pair block is zero. If only the reference is empty, the target block remains valid and the reference/pair blocks are zero. Single-point covariance/extents are zero. Undefined relation margins are zero with a zero mask. No fallback origin, stale centroid, NaN, or variable dimension is allowed.

## Frozen observation likelihood

Post-action visible geometry is reduced to one relation-specific signed predicate score:

- `inside`: the minimum of centered inclusion-fraction margin and normalized robust-box boundary margin;
- `on`: the minimum of horizontal-overlap, vertical-gap, and temporal support-stability margins.

Visibility bins are missing (either cloud under 16 points), 16–31, 32–63, and at least 64 points. For each non-missing relation/bin, a univariate 32-bin class-conditional histogram is fitted on training task families only, with pooled equal-frequency edges, duplicate-edge merging, and Laplace smoothing \(\alpha=1\). The missing-bin LLR is exactly zero. The table is frozen and identical for all transition variants.

## Frozen aliasing population

On validation data, the observation-only filter selects:

- a retract threshold \(\theta^-_r\) maximizing invalidation TPR subject to false retraction rate at most 5%;
- a continue threshold \(\theta^+_r\) maximizing valid-event continuation subject to

\[
\mathrm{FCR}=\frac{\#\{invalid\ events\ assigned\ continue\}}{\#\{invalid\ events\}}\le0.05.
\]

Always-non-retract and always-non-continue candidates guarantee feasibility. Both thresholds are frozen. The claim-bearing aliasing population is

\[
\mathcal A_r=\{o:\theta^-_r<q^{obs}(o)<\theta^+_r\}.
\]

Membership never uses a test label. Its size, prevalence, task coverage, and visibility composition must be reported; the full distribution is supporting evidence.

## Transition head and calibration

Trace sequences are uniformly resampled or padded to 16 steps with a validity mask. A two-layer temporal 1D convolution (64 channels, kernel 3) and masked mean pooling are concatenated with \(q_t\), the 54D geometry vector, and a fixed `inside/on` token. A two-layer 128-unit GELU MLP emits one validity logit. The entire trainable mechanism must remain below 100k parameters.

Training uses prevalence-preserving shuffled batches and ordinary binary NLL. A two-parameter affine logit calibration \(a\ell+b\) is fitted on validation NLL and frozen. If a shared `inside/on` head requires relation-specific branches, the first study falls back to `inside` rather than adding modules.

## Causal data requirements

Physics-mediated E1 rollouts restore identical pre-states. Preserved and invalidated outcomes must overlap in command parameters, and at least one action family must produce both outcomes through interaction-state variation. Sampling is matched by action family, duration, pre-state geometry, and post-observation visibility. Standardized mean difference above 0.1 triggers rematching or propensity weighting. A command-only shortcut classifier above 60% balanced accuracy rejects the stratum.

Trace shuffling is only within task, action family, duration, pre-state geometry, and visibility strata. Action templates and task families used for test are held out.

E2 uses newly collected on-policy StarVLA/π0.5 chunks or exact original-state replay. Replaying a chunk in a different restored state is prohibited. Scripted physics evidence cannot be promoted to a VLA-distribution claim.

## Comparisons

1. POT-style observation LLR plus persistence prior;
2. learned no-trace PointMap transition prior;
3. within-stratum shuffled \(\tau^{kin}\);
4. command-only \(\tau^{cmd}\);
5. primary realized-kinematic \(\tau^{kin}\);
6. appendix feedback-inclusive \(\tau^{fb}\);
7. matched RGB/visual-feature verifier;
8. simulator-geometry oracle.

The E0 diagnostic separately compares object-addressed RGB, complete two-frame depth, sparse object PointMap, and simulator pose. It tests representation/headroom only and cannot support the transition claim.

## Primary statistic and gates

Each variant receives its own validation-only retraction threshold at the common false-retraction budget. For invalidity score \(s=1-q\), choose the candidate with greatest validation TPR subject to FPR≤5%; freeze before test.

The primary statistic is

\[
\mathrm{IMR@FRR5}=1-\frac{\#\{invalid\ events\ retracted\}}{\#\{invalid\ events\}},
\quad
\mathrm{FRR}=\frac{\#\{valid\ events\ retracted\}}{\#\{valid\ events\}}\le0.05.
\]

Re-observe counts as an invalidation miss for this binary statistic. The unit is one historical ledger update. Report achieved FRR without interpolation and 95% task-family cluster-bootstrap confidence intervals. Three-way decision cost, risk-coverage, NLL/Brier/ECE, latency, and full-distribution results are supporting metrics.

On \(\mathcal A_r\), \(\tau^{kin}\) must reduce IMR@FRR5 by at least 10% relative and by at least 5 percentage points absolute versus the strongest observation/no-trace baseline, while achieved test FRR remains at most 5%. It must beat command-only, lose its incremental gain under within-stratum shuffling, transfer to held-out actions/tasks, survive PointMaps produced from deployed learned depth and instance masks, and improve closed-loop typed rollback/final success after the oracle recovery gate.

If the gate fails, the exact conclusion is:

> Execution-trace conditional value was not established on the preregistered aliasing population.

## Evidence ladder and complexity boundary

- E0: controlled representation, label, observability, and rollback-headroom diagnostics;
- E1: matched physics-mediated action effects;
- E2: exact-state/on-policy VLA action distribution;
- E3: closed-loop typed rollback after oracle and offline gates.

The VLA and perception teachers remain frozen. The study uses one deterministic PointMap summary, one sub-100k transition head, one frozen univariate observation table, validation-only affine calibration, and per-variant operating thresholds. It adds no RGB-D video decoder, diffusion model, LLM planner, learned recovery policy, or `grasp/open` claim.
