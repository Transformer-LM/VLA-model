## Installed domain skills

- OpenPI, OpenVLA-OFT, and Cosmos Policy references live in their installed Skill directories.
- Use their current official upstream repositories and documentation for mutable APIs, checkpoints, datasets, and hardware requirements.

## Source discipline

- Prefer papers, official repositories, official documentation, and benchmark definitions.
- Record paper/repository version or access date for mutable claims.
- Distinguish author-released code, community reproduction, and unofficial implementation.
- Verify benchmark splits, action representations, data volume, and evaluation protocol before comparing numbers.
